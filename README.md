🌊 ResQ AI
AI-Powered Disaster Intelligence & Emergency Response Platform for Kerala

📌 Problem Statement
Kerala frequently faces floods, landslides, heavy rainfall, and other natural disasters during the monsoon season. Existing disaster response systems are fragmented, making it difficult for citizens to report incidents, authorities to coordinate rescue operations, and volunteers to respond efficiently.

💡 Solution
ResQ AI is an intelligent disaster management platform that enables citizens, volunteers, and authorities to collaborate in real time using AI-powered disaster analysis, live incident tracking, and resource management.
